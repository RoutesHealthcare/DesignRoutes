The built editor (`dist/DesignRoutes-editor.html`) contains the software and data below as well as Routes

## Map data

- World countries: Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:110m admin 0 countries. Public domain.
- France's departments: Natural Earth, 1:10m admin 1 states and provinces. Public domain.
- UK regions: Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0
  (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
  Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2025.
  (International Territorial Level 1, January 2025, ultra generalised clipped boundaries.)
- Route maps' areas and roads: Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence
  v.3.0 (International Territorial Level 2, January 2025, and Local Authority Districts, May 2025, generalised
  clipped boundaries). Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2025 and 2026
